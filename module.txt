# Chapter 1: The Cursed Energy System

*"Sorcerers, Cursed Spirits, it doesn’t matter. The only ones who are able to transcend beyond being merely strong are those with overwhelming aggression."*

This chapter replaces the standard 5e magic system entirely. Translate 5e references using the rules below.

## The CE Point System

All sorcerers, cursed vessels, and cursed spirits operate on Cursed Energy Points (CE Points) rather than spell slots. Your class determines your CE Point maximum, as shown on each class table. CE Points recover fully on a long rest unless your race or class specifies otherwise. On a short rest, you may meditate for at least 10 minutes and recover CE Points equal to your CE Stat modifier + your proficiency bonus.

***CE Stat.*** Each class designates a primary CE Stat used for technique saving throw DCs, technique attack bonuses, and CE-related checks:

CE Save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your CE Stat modifier

CE Attack Bonus = your proficiency bonus + your CE Stat modifier

##### Cursed Energy Stat by Class

| Class | CE Stat |
|:---|:---|
| Warrior/Barbarian | Constitution |
| Combatant/Fighter | Strength |
| Striker/Monk | Dexterity |
| Chanter/Bard | Charisma |

## CE Grades & Character Tiers

The jujutsu world ranks sorcerers and curses alike. Use the following table as a character tier and narrative framework. Mission assignments, faction respect, and enemy Challenge Rating (CR) should scale accordingly.

Classes may alter the number of CE points available to players. It's also possible for a creature with a different number of CE points to be a higher or lower grade than the table would otherwise suggest.


##### Level, Grade, and Approximate CE Points

| Level | CE Points Total | Sorcerer Grade |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 1-4 | 6-10 | Grade 4 |
| 5-8 | 12-16 | Grade 3 |
| 9-12 | 18-24 | Grade 2 |
| 13-16 | 26-32 | Grade 1 |
| 17-20 | 35-45 | Special Grade |

## Cursed Energy Perception

All player characters with CE access have Cursed Sight as a passive ability, with the exception of those with a Heavenly Restriction. Within 30 feet, they automatically detect the presence of active cursed energy and cursed spirits without rolling. It does not automatically reveal creatures that are hidden or supressing their CE. At greater distances or through solid material, they may make a Perception check using their CE Stat modifier instead of Wisdom.

Non-sorcerers and civilians cannot perceive cursed spirits at all unless under the effect of a technique that grants CE sight or use of a cursed-tool.

> ##### Workarounds
> Players with a Heavenly Restriction either require a cursed tool that grants Cursed Sight or the Complete Heavenly Restriction feat. Without this, they roll attacks at disadvantage against any opponent that requires Cursed Sight to observe.

## Binding Vows

A Binding Vow is a metaphysical contract declared aloud and enforced by the logic of cursed energy itself. Sorcerers - and even curses - may enter Binding Vows to exchange limitations for power. A Vow does not require a second party; a sorcerer may bind themselves.

***Making a Vow***. As an action, declare the Vow aloud. It takes effect immediately. The DM must approve the power gain and confirm the limitation is meaningful and testable.

***Breaking a Vow***. The penalty for breaking a self-imposed binding vow is the loss of whatever benefit was gained through the contract, though unbreakable binding vows that permanently adjust a user's abilities can be made at the DM's discretion.

##### Example Binding Vows
| Vow | Limitation | Power Gained |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Reveal your technique to your opponent | Opponent knows exactly what you can do | Technique output doubles this encounter |
| Never use your Domain on non-sorcerers | Domain is unusable on curses and civilians | Domain hits guaranteed; no Simple Domain counter |
| Fight without CE infusion on attacks | Cannot spend CE on attack rolls | Gain 10 temp CE Points for technique-only use |
| Always fight to kill, never subdue | Cannot choose to knock unconscious | +2 to all CE attack rolls permanently |

See the Binding Vow Jurist and Perfectly Average Binding Vow feats for other applications.

\page

## Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT)

Positive energy is the product of multiplying two negative (cursed) energies. It can heal wounds and destroy curses. Mastering RCT requires a CE Stat of 16 or higher, or the RCT Adept feat.

***Healing with RCT.*** As an action, spend 4 CE Points per d8 of healing you wish to apply to yourself or a creature you touch.

***RCT Against Curses.*** When used offensively against a cursed spirit, RCT energy is treated as radiant damage, to which most cursed spirits are vulnerable (double damage unless otherwise stated in the stat block). 

***Usage in Combat.*** Application is done using the same method as a CE-infused attack. If you are able to use RCT, you may specify when you attack whether you are using RCT or not.

***RCT cannot save the already dead.*** RCT heals wounds, regenerates severed limbs, and purges poisons, but it cannot restore a creature reduced to 0 hit points by a soul-level attack (such as Mahito's Idle Transfiguration on a critical save failure).

## Black Flash

When a sorcerer lands a CE-infused attack, there is a rare moment when the distortion of cursed energy perfectly coincides with the physical impact - a Black Flash. This is not a technique so it cannot be trained directly. It can only be achieved.

### Triggering a Black Flash

A Black Flash can only be triggered on a natural 20 on the attack roll (or a natural 19 if you have the Heightened Flash feat - see the Feats chapter). After confirming the natural 20, you must then make a secondary CE Stat check (d20 + your CE Stat modifier). The DC for this check is 20.

- On a success (20 or higher): The Black Flash lands. The attack deals bonus damage equal to 2d6 per CE Point spent on the infused strike as force damage, in addition to all other damage. This damage ignores resistance.
- On a failure: The attack still hits and deals normal critical hit damage, but the Black Flash does not trigger.
  
### Flow State

Achieving a Black Flash puts you into a Flow State until the end of combat or until you fail a CE Stat check by 3 or more. While in a Flow State:
- The DC for subsequent Black Flash checks is reduced to 17 instead of 20 (or 16 with the Heightened Flash feat).
- You have advantage on all CE-related ability checks.
- You may add your CE Stat modifier to the damage of all CE-infused attacks, even without spending CE Points on them.

# Chapter 2: Cursed Techniques

*"With a proper foundation, imagination, and a little sense it only takes a slight trigger to change a person."*

The following chapter details Inate and General techniques, and how they function in this module.

## How Techniques Work

Techniques are innate, always-available special abilities. They do not occupy slots - they cost CE Points from your pool. There are two main types of techniques in this module:
- Inate Techniques
- General Techniques

> ##### Techniques and Saving Throws
> When a technique requires a saving throw, the DC is always:
>
> CE Save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your CE Stat modifier
>
> Unless the technique specifies otherwise, creatures make the save at the start of their next turn if an effect is ongoing, and at the end of their turn if the effect was applied to them on their own turn.
> :

### Inate Techniques
Even among sorcerers, it is rare to possess enough intrinsic talent to be born with an innate technique. Those born with one can train theirs to be stronger even if it is considered to be weak once they first learn to use it; advanced applications called Extensions and Reversals are developed through training and maturity.

Inate Techniques are non-transferable. They are congenitally engraved into the user when they are born. A sorcerer who witnesses another's Inate Technique may study its CE pattern (see Technique Thief feat), but they cannot replicate the underlying structure.

***Lapse vs. Extension vs. Reversal.*** A Lapse technique is the standard forward application - the technique doing what it was born to do. Extension Techniques are attacks, spells, or abilities derived from the user's innate technique. A Reversal is the positive-CE inversion of the same technique, and requires RCT proficiency to use. Reversals often have healing or regenerative applications, but can also be offensive in unexpected ways.

### General Techniques
Sorcerers can also learn General Techniques - maniupulation of cursed energy seperate from an Inate Technique. See the table on the next page.

## Building a Custom Technique

Every player character should have an innate technique that reflects their soul. The following framework ensures custom techniques are balanced, thematically correct, and mechanically interesting.

### Design Principles

***Root in an idea.*** It's helpful to base an Inate Technique on a concept or idea. Your technique should answer: *what does this person carry inside them that has given cursed energy a shape?*

***Define the Lapse first.*** The standard application. This is what your technique does when it does the obvious thing. Keep it to one clear effect.

***Define the Restriction.*** Every technique has a cost beyond CE Points. What does yours cost that isn't just points? This can also be a situational application such as requiring both hands to activate Boogie Woogie.

***Scale the cost.*** The following points are a guide to the cost and level of the abilities of a technique.
- Basic effect (1 target, minor condition or 1d8–2d8 damage): 2–3 CE Points. Grade 4/3.
- Significant effect (3d8+, restrain, area): 4–6 CE Points. Grade 2.
- Catastrophic output: 8–12 CE Points. Grade 1.
- Domain activation is always 15 CE Points. Special Grade.

***Consider the Reversal.*** When positive CE is applied to your technique's logic, what happens? For example, Infinity reversed becomes infinite attraction/Blue. Not every technique has a clean reversal, and that's fine. It can be developed through play, not designed from the start. Just keep it in mind.

##### Technique Move Costs

| Tier | CE Point Cost | Typical Effect |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Basic | 2–3 | 1d8–3d8 damage, minor condition, small utility |
| Standard | 4–6 | 4d8–6d8 damage, significant conditions, area effects |
| Advanced Extension | 7–10 | 7d8+ damage, powerful conditions, terrain effects |
| Maximum Output | 11–14 | Catastrophic damage, near-instant conditions |
| Domain Expansion | 15 | Separate chapter |

Players with an Inate Technique *must* use a custom technique unless they are a clan sorcerer or have the Clan Outcast feat.

## Example Inate Techniques

### Boogie Woogie
***2 CE Points per activation.***

By clapping your hands, you instantaneously swap the positions of any two targets within range.

***Lapse.*** As an action or bonus action, clap your hands. Choose two visible creatures or objects within 30 feet (at least one must have measurable CE, or be you). They instantly exchange positions.

***No Save.*** This technique does not allow a saving throw. Creatures moved into a dangerous location (open flame, off a ledge, into a grapple) may make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 14) to resist only if they are aware of the specific danger in advance. Their position is still swapped, but they may avoid the damage on success.
:
Potential combat applications include:
- Swap a flying enemy directly into the ground (they take fall damage)
- Swap an ally out of a grapple or dangerous position
- Swap two enemies to disrupt formations and trigger flanking
- Swap yourself behind a target before your attack roll (gain advantage)

Once per turn, a Boogie Woogie swap performed immediately before a melee attack grants advantage on that attack roll.

### Cursed Speech
***Variable cost.***

Every command you speak is reinforced with cursed energy, forcing other's obedience at the expense of your own body. Overuse destroys your vocal cords.

***Lapse - Command (2 CE).*** As a bonus action, shout a single one-word command (Flee, Kneel, Stop, Fall, etc.). Targets that can hear you within 30 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw or obey for 1 round. This functions as the *Command* spell.

***Extension - Binding Word (4 CE).*** As an action, issue a complex command. One target within 30 feet makes a Wisdom saving throw or is compelled to follow the command for up to 1 minute (concentration).

***Roar (8 CE).*** As an action, release a full-force CE shout in a 30-foot cone. All creatures in the cone make a Constitution saving throw or take 6d8 thunder damage and are knocked prone. On success: half damage, not prone. After using Roar, you cannot speak (including using Cursed Speech) until you complete a short rest.

***Physical Cost.*** Each use of Cursed Speech deals 1 point of damage to you (recovered on a long rest). If your HP reaches 0 through this damage, your vocal cords are permanently destroyed and Cursed Speech is lost forever.

\column

### Ratio Technique
***2 CE Points per activation.***

You perceive the divine proportion within any physical body or object - a point where structure is weakest at exactly the 7:3 ratio.

***Lapse.*** As part of a melee attack, spend 2 CE Points. If the attack hits, you mark the target's Ratio Point. Until the end of your next turn, any attack made against that point is an automatic critical hit (regardless of the d20 roll).

***Extension - Collapse (5 CE Points).*** Apply the ratio to a structure (wall, floor, vehicle, ceiling). The designated point collapses violently. All creatures within 10 feet of the collapse make a Dexterity saving throw or take 6d8 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone (half damage, not prone on a save).

### Projection Sorcery
***4 CE Points per use.***

You experience the world in 24 frames per second of deliberate motion, and can pre-program each frame.

***Lapse.*** As part of your turn, pre-plan your movement and attacks in 24 segments. You may make a melee attack at any point during your movement, splitting move-attack-move freely, without provoking opportunity attacks. Any creature in your path must make a Dexterity saving throw or take 3d8 force damage and be stunned until the start of their next turn.

***Shutter.*** Any creature that is stunned, restrained, or otherwise rendered immobile *by you* is frozen in a "frame." You may make one additional attack against them as a reaction this round.

***Restriction.*** If your planned movement is interrupted (forcibly stopped, terrain blocked), you are frozen until the start of your next turn. While frozen: AC 8, no reactions, no movement.

### Blood Manipulation
***Variable cost.***

You govern the flow of blood - your own and others'. Applications scale with CE Points spent.

***Piercing Blood (2 CE).*** Ranged CE attack, range 60 feet. On a hit, 3d8 piercing damage. This attack ignores armour made of non-cursed material.

***Crimson Binding (4 CE).*** Concentration, up to 1 minute. Blood erupts from existing wounds. Target makes a Strength saving throw or is restrained. They may repeat the save at the end of each of their turns.

***Convergence (6 CE).*** Concentration, up to 1 minute. You accelerate your own circulation to superhuman levels. Gain advantage on all attack rolls, your movement speed doubles, and all your attacks deal +2d6 damage. You take 1d6 hit point damage at the start of each of your turns.

***Supernova (8 CE).*** All creatures within 30 feet of you make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure: 8d8 necrotic damage. On a successful save, only 4d8.

\page

### Infinity
***Passive. Always active.***

The space between the user and any incoming attack contains infinite divisions. Physical objects and CE converge on your position asymptotically but never fully arrive.

***Passive Effect.*** You are immune to all ranged attacks and have resistance to all melee attacks. Creatures making melee attacks against you must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + PB + Wisdom modifier) or the attack is reduced to zero effect and their movement ends 5 feet from you. On a success, the attack deals half its normal damage.

***Drain.*** Infinity costs 3 CE Points per hour of active combat. It deactivates when you are unconscious.

***Extension - Red (8 CE Points).*** Amplified repulsion. As an action, emit a 60-foot line of obliteration. Creatures in the line make a Strength saving throw or are pushed 30 feet and take 8d10 force damage (half on save).

***Reversal - Blue (5 CE Points).*** Amplified attraction. As an action, create a pulling force in a 30-foot cone. Creatures in the cone make a Strength saving throw or are pulled up to 20 feet toward you and take 4d8 force damage (half on save).

***Combination - Purple (20 CE Points, once per long rest).*** Combine Blue and Red into a hollow sphere of erasure. Choose a point within 120 feet. Everything within a 30-foot sphere of that point makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 22) or is erased - reduced to 0 HP and suffering automatic death save failure. On a success: 12d10 force damage. This is a Special Grade technique.

### Idle Transfiguration
***3 CE Points per target.***

You touch the soul of another and reshape it, making the physical form conform to a distorted version of what it could be.

***Lapse.*** As an action, you touch a humanoid. They make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, choose one transfiguration effect:
- Speed reduced to half and AC reduced by 4
- All attacks deal half damage
- One sense is lost (sight, hearing, or touch)
- The target begins leaking CE uncontrollably: all allies within 5 feet take 1d6 necrotic damage at the start of each round

This transfiguration is permanent until removed by RCT (requiring 8 CE Points from a Grade 1 or higher sorcerer).

***Mass Transfiguration (12 CE Points, once per long rest).*** Affect all creatures within 30 feet simultaneously. Each makes the Wisdom saving throw independently. Use of this ability marks the caster as a Disaster-level threat. Jujutsu High will respond accordingly.

***Polymorphic Soul Isomer (6 CE Points).*** Sculpt previously transfigured humans into soldiers. They function as Zombies (Monster Manual) with +4 to all attacks and AC 14. You may control a number of soldiers equal to your Charisma modifier. They act on your initiative.

### Ten Shadows Technique
***3–10 CE Points per shikigami.***

You summon divine shikigami - powerful beings bound in shadow - using hand signs. You begin knowing two shikigami (Divine Dogs and Nue). Additional shikigami are unlocked by defeating and absorbing them.

# Chapter 3: Domain Expansions

*"You're underestimating yourself and others instead of envisioning yourself as being stronger in the near future."*

## Domain Rules

Domain Expansion is the pinnacle of jujutsu. It's a technique that manifests the user's innate technique as a separate reality. The mechanical benefit of a Domain is guaranteed accuracy: inside a Domain, the user's innate technique strikes with absolute certainty, and targets automatically fail saving throws against it.

### Activating a Domain

Cost: 15 CE Points + concentration (maximum 1 minute, or longer with Extended Domain feat).

Size: Creates a sphere of 60-foot radius centred on the caster. All creatures within the sphere are subject to the guaranteed-accuracy clause unless they have a Simple Domain or an active Domain of their own.

Action: Activating a Domain Expansion requires your full action.

### Domain vs. Domain

If a second Domain Expansion is activated within an already-active Domain, both users make a contested roll. The lower total's Domain is suppressed. On a tie, both Domains collapse.

Alternatively, a sorcerer with a significantly more refined Domain (DM discretion - generally a full tier of CE Grade above the other) may not need to roll as their Domain would overwrite the other.

> ##### Domain Clash: Quick Reference
> When two Domains clash, use the following procedure:

> 1. Both users roll d20 + CE Stat modifier + proficiency bonus simultaneously.
> 2. The lower total's Domain is suppressed immediately.
> 3. On a tie: both Domains collapse. Both users are stunned until the start of their next turn.
> 4. A Domain that is suppressed may be re-activated on the user's next turn for 15 CE Points.
> 5. If a user's Domain is suppressed three times in a single combat session, they cannot activate it again until a long rest.

### Simple Domain

A Simple Domain is a smaller, defensive CE bubble that neutralises the guaranteed-accuracy clause for the creature inside it. See the Simple Domain feat. It does not expel the user from the enemy Domain - they remain inside, but they are no longer subject to the automatic-hit effect.
\column
### Barrier-Only Domain

Spending 8 CE Points creates a closed Domain with no innate technique effect - a CE barrier of 30-foot radius with AC 18 and 100 hit points. It blocks CE detection from outside and prevents non-CE-based intrusion. It does not grant guaranteed accuracy.

> ##### A Note on Domain Frequency
> Domain Expansion is a single-use, high-stakes tool. Most sorcerers use it once per encounter at most. The CE cost (15 Points) and concentration requirement mean it cannot be maintained carelessly.
> :

## Example Domain Expansions

### Unlimited Void

***Domain Effect.*** All creatures other than Gojo within the Domain are incapacitated for the duration. All their saving throws automatically fail. On a CE saving throw (DC 22) at the Domain's end, creatures that fail are stunned for 1d6 multiplied by 10 minutes upon leaving. Non-sorcerer civilians exposed for more than 10 seconds suffer permanent psychic damage.

***Counter Difficulty.*** Unlimited Void is widely considered the most powerful Domain in the current era. Simple Domain provides standard protection, but no Domain in living memory has successfully overwritten it in a direct clash.

***Domain Effect.*** All creatures other than Jogo take 2d6 fire damage at the start of each of their turns from the ambient temperature alone. Fire resistance is suppressed for all non-Jogo creatures within the Domain. Water-based effects are immediately evaporated.

***Disaster Flames.*** Jogo's technique attacks within the Domain are guaranteed to hit. His Disaster Flames (ranged CE attack, 5d8 fire, 60-foot range) are resolved without attack rolls.

***Open Domain.*** Unlike standard Domains, Malevolent Shrine is open; it does not create a barrier. Simple Domain can reduce (but not eliminate) the guaranteed-accuracy effect. A Simple Domain user must still contend with the physical attacks.

The following species replace standard D&D races for this module. Each represents a distinct relationship to cursed energy and the jujutsu world.

## Human Sorcerer

The majority of jujutsu practitioners are human - rare individuals born with the ability to perceive and manipulate cursed energy. Most inherit their innate technique from a sorcerer bloodline; clan membership shapes access to training, resources, and social standing. Independent sorcerers exist, but they operate without institutional support and are treated with suspicion by the major clans.

Humans are defined by their adaptability. Their CE ceilings are theoretically uncapped, and history's most powerful sorcerers - Gojo Satoru, Geto Suguru, Yuki Tsukumo - have been human.

### Human Sorcerer Traits

As a human sorcerer, you have the following traits:

***Age.*** Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and typically live less than a century.

***Speed.*** Your base movement speed is 30 feet.

***Alignment.*** Human sorcerers span the full moral range. Institutional sorcerers tend toward law; independents toward neutrality; curse users toward chaos.

***Size.*** Your choice of small or medium.

***Adaptable CE.*** Once per long rest, when you fail a CE-related ability check or saving throw, you may reroll and take the higher result.

***Baseline Sorcerer.*** Choose one Grade 4 Technique from the General Technique list.

***Skill Versatility.*** You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice.

***Languages.*** You speak Japanese and one additional language of your choice.

***Sub-categories.*** Choose between Normal Sorcerer, Clan Sorcerer, Heavenly Restriction or Cursed Vessel.

#### Normal Sorcerer

You are a "normal" sorcerer. To some, you're common. *Weak*. But there are people who rise above those preconceptions. Will you be one of them?

***Ability Score Increase.*** One ability score of your choice increases by 2, and a different one increases by 1. Alternatively, three ability scores of your choice each increase by 1.

***Self-Taught Technique.*** Choose one Grade 3 Technique from the General Technique list in addition to the one granted to you by *Baseline Sorcerer*.

***Adaptive Growth.*** Whenever you complete a long rest after surviving a combat encounter against an enemy whose CR exceeded your level, gain 1 Evolution Point (maximum equal to proficiency bonus). As a bonus action, you may spend an Evolution Point to:

- Reduce a technique's CE cost by 2
- Add your proficiency bonus to damage

***Ability Score Increase.*** One ability score of your choice increases by 2, and a different one increases by 1. Alternatively, three ability scores of your choice each increase by 1.

***Clan Technique.*** If you are from a named sorcerer clan (Zenin, Gojo, Kamo, etc.), you begin play knowing a technique from your clan's lineage. Coordinate with your DM to develop this technique if it's not defined in the module.

***Family Secrets.*** At 5th level, choose one:
- Learn one additional technique from the General Technique table at your current grade value
- Reduce all clan technique CE costs by 1 (minimum 1)
- Gain advantage on Domain clashes

***Clan Burden.*** Your name carries weight. You have advantage on social checks involving sorcerers who recognize your clan and disadvantage on Deception checks against them.

#### Heavenly Restriction 

Some individuals are born with little to no cursed energy and in the jujutsu world, this is perhaps the most dangerous thing a person can be. Without CE, the body compensates.

***Ability Score Increase.*** One physical ability score (Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution) of your choice increases by 3. A second physical ability score of your choice increases by 1.

***Ability Score Maximum.*** Your chosen primary physical ability score has a maximum of 24 instead of 20.

***Speed.*** Your base movement speed is 35 feet.

***Heavenly Restriction.*** There are three seperate tiers of Heavenly Restriction. Choose one.

* ***Barely Restricted.*** Your cursed energy is stunted but present. Your CE Point maximum is reduced by one quarter (rounded up), and you cannot learn Reverse Cursed Technique.

* ***Partial Restriction.*** Your cursed energy is severely diminished. Your CE Point maximum is reduced by half (rounded up), you cannot learn Reverse Cursed Technique, and you cannot use any technique that costs more than 4 CE Points. In exchange, increase one physical ability score of your choice by 2, to a maximum of 22.

* ***Fully Restricted.*** You have 0 CE Points and cannot activate CE-based abilities or techniques. In place of Cursed Energy Points, you have Physical Energy Points equal to half of your CE Point maximum (rounded up) which can only be used to activate melee-based or self-affecting techniques. You cannot benefit from CE-based healing (RCT). You cannot be detected by Cursed Sight as you have no CE signature. Curses cannot perceive you passively. You do not have an Inate Technique. In exchange, increase two physical ability scores of your choice by 2 each, to a maximum of 24.

***Superhuman Body.*** Your jump height and distance are doubled. You take no damage from falls of 30 feet or less, and half damage from falls up to 60 feet. You do not require sleep; 4 hours of rest provides the full benefits of a long rest.

***Perfect Muscle.*** When you make a Strength check or Strength saving throw and the result is less than your Strength score, you may use your Strength score as the result instead.

## Cursed Corpse

Cursed corpses are defined as inanimate objects possessed by a curse that is capable of standing on its own. These types of curses that appear naturally are hostile to humans. Most Cursed Corpses are tools, but an abrupt mutation in the construction process produced a Cursed Corpse with genuine consciousness, personality, and three distinct cores representing different combat aspects.

### Cursed Corpse Traits

***Ability Score Increase.*** One ability score of your choice increases by 2, and a different one increases by 1. Alternatively, three ability scores of your choice each increase by 1.

***Age.*** Cursed Corpses do not age biologically. You are as old as your construction date (unless operated remotely, then age as human).

***Speed.*** Your base movement speed is 30 feet.

***Alignment.*** Most Abrupt Mutations, having developed genuine personalities, trend toward good alignments and strong personal ethics, but there are exceptions to every rule.

***Size.*** Your choice of small, medium, or large.

***Independent Soul.*** You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice.

***Constructed Nature.*** You do not need to eat, breathe, or sleep. You are immune to poison, disease, and the exhausted condition. 4 hours of powered-down rest provides the benefits of a long rest.

***Tough Hide.*** While unarmoured, your AC equals 13 + your Constitution modifier.

***Languages.*** Japanese.

***Sub-categories.*** Choose between Abrupt Mutation or Combat Model.

#### Abrupt Mutation

***Disarming Presence.*** Despite everything, you have advantage on Persuasion checks with students, children, and creatures the DM determines are inexplicably charmed by you. This happens more than you would expect.

***Three Cores.*** You have three cursed cores, each representing a different combat mode. You begin each day in your default mode. As a bonus action, you may shift between the following modes. You may only switch modes once per turn.

- Default. No modifiers. You are as you have always been.
- Attack Mode. Your Strength-based attack and damage rolls gain a +2 bonus, and you have advantage on Athletics checks. You have distadvantage on all Intelligence based checks.
- Defense Mode. Your AC increases by 2. Your speed decreases by 10 feet and you cannot take the Dash action.

***Core Damage.*** If a targeted attack destroys one of your cores, you lose access to that mode until you spend a long rest repairing it with 4 CE Points.

***Adaptive Core.*** At the end of each long rest, one core of your choice gains advantage on its first save or attack.

Much like the Puppet Manipulation technique of Mechamaru, you are humanoid in form but far from a human. You were built as a weapon - either entirely autonomous, or piloted remotely, this *is* your body, for better or worse.

## Cursed Painting

Cursed Paintings are the result of a forbidden experiment - cursed spirits merged with human beings before birth, producing something that is neither entirely curse nor entirely human. They are alive in a way that pure curses are not, and inhuman in a way that pure humans can never be. Their blood, when spilled, does not behave like ordinary blood.

### Cursed Painting Traits

***Ability Score Increase.*** One ability score of your choice increases by 2, and a different one increases by 1. Alternatively, three ability scores of your choice each increase by 1.

***Age.*** As human.

***Alignment.*** Cursed Paintings carry the emotional inheritance of both their human and curse halves. They tend toward neutrality, shaped more by experience than instinct.

***Size.*** Your choice of small, medium or large.

***Speed.*** Your base movement speed is 30 feet.

***Half-Human Nature.*** Unlike pure cursed spirits, you can be perceived by non-sorcerers and exist openly in the human world without concealment. You register as human to casual Cursed Sight, though a sorcerer who studies your CE signature closely may make an CE check (DC 14) to identify your true nature.

***Blood Technique.*** Your blood carries concentrated cursed energy. When you are dealt slashing or piercing damage, you may use your reaction to direct the blood as a CE attack. Make a ranged CE attack against the creature that damaged you (+CE Attack Bonus to hit, range 30 ft.). On a hit, deal 2d6 piercing damage. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

***Reconstruct.*** When reduced to 0 hit points, you may spend 6 CE Points as a reaction to instantly stabilise and regain 1 hit point. Once per long rest.

***Inhuman Threshold.*** Your CE Point maximum increases by 2.

***Distorted Presence.*** You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed. Creatures that rely on purely biological senses (scent, tremorsense) have disadvantage on checks to track or locate you.

***Darkvision.*** 60 feet.

***Languages.*** Japanese and one additional language of your choice.

***Sub-categories.*** Choose between Human Aligned or Curse Aligned.

#### Human Aligned

Your human inheritance is the louder voice. You feel hunger even if you do not need to eat. You dream. You form attachments in the way humans do, with all the vulnerability that entails. Other sorcerers find you easier to trust. Cursed spirits find you contemptible.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Your Charisma score increases by 1 in addition to your base *Ability Score Increase*.

***Living Warmth.*** You count as humanoid for all purposes, even when targeted by effects that distinguish between humanoids and cursed spirits. Healing from RCT affects you at full value.

***Human CE Reserves.*** Your CE recovers as a human sorcerer's does. Fully recover CE Points on a long rest, and partially on a short rest. Additionally, once per long rest when you finish a short rest, you may recover additional CE Points equal to your Constitution modifier.

***Sorcerer Recognition.*** Jujutsu High and institutional sorcerers treat you as a registered sorcerer rather than a cursed spirit. You have advantage on Persuasion checks when dealing with human sorcerers who are aware of your nature.

***Emotional Anchor.*** When you fail a saving throw against being frightened or charmed, you may reroll the save once and take the higher result. This does not stack with Distorted Presence, which should be applied first.

#### Curse Aligned

Your curse half is the louder voice. You do not dream. Attachment feels like a concept you understand intellectually but cannot fully access. You are faster, stranger, and more dangerous than your human-aligned kin and cursed spirits recognise something of themselves in you, even if they cannot place it.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Your Strength score increases by 1 in addition to your base *Ability Score Increase*.

***Curse Recognition.*** You are perceived as a cursed spirit by Cursed Sight. Cursed spirits do not automatically treat you as a target and will often hesitate before attacking, allowing you to attempt a Charisma check (DC determined by the spirit's grade) to communicate or negotiate.

***Accelerated Reconstruction.*** Your Reconstruct feature costs 4 CE Points instead of 6.

***Curse Body.*** You have resistance to necrotic damage. When you take necrotic damage, you may convert up to half of it into temporary CE Points (maximum equal to your proficiency bonus) that last until the end of your next turn.

***Bloodline Density.*** Your Blood Technique deals 2d8 piercing damage instead of 2d6, and the range increases to 60 feet.

## Sentient Cursed Spirit

The most powerful cursed spirits develop language, philosophy, and purpose - transcending the mindless hunger of lesser curses to become thinking, speaking beings with agendas. Mahito, Hanami, Dagon, and Jogo are examples: curses that want something, that argue about humanity, that have aesthetic preferences and favourite weather.

Playing a Sentient Cursed Spirit is a DM-approved option for campaigns that explore the Disaster Curses' perspective, or for players who want to embody the antagonist side of the jujutsu world.

### Sentient Cursed Spirit Traits

![mahito](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/jujutsu-kaisen/images/4/4e/Mahito_%28Anime%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/268?cb=20240618013419) {width:180px,margin-right:-0.6cm,wrapLeft}

***Age.*** Cursed spirits do not age. They persist until exorcised.

***Alignment.*** Most Sentient Cursed Spirits are chaotic. A rare few develop a self-imposed code of ethics, but it rarely resembles human morality.

***Size.*** Medium (or as defined by your spirit's concept).

***Speed.*** Your base movement speed is 35 feet.

***Darkvision.*** 60 feet.

***Cursed Origin.*** You are immune to poison and necrotic damage. You take double damage from Reverse Cursed Technique (positive energy). You take full damage from CE-infused attacks, and you have no resistance to weapons that have been cursed or infused by a sorcerer.

***Phase.*** As a bonus action, you may become incorporeal until the start of your next turn. While incorporeal, you can pass through solid objects but cannot end your movement inside one. Your attacks and CE abilities still function normally while phased, and you can only be hit by CE-infused attacks.

***CE Hunger.*** You must absorb CE from defeated enemies or cursed objects to sustain yourself. Once per week, you must absorb CE equivalent to at least 10 CE Points worth of energy (defeating a sorcerer, consuming a cursed tool, or absorbing residual CE from a battlefield). Failure to feed causes one level of exhaustion.

***Unbounded Malice.*** You have advantage on Intimidation checks. You cannot be charmed by non-CE attempts.

***Languages.*** Japanese (instinctive). Some older spirits speak archaic dialects or no language at all.

***Sub-categories.*** Choose between Natural Spirit or Cursed Animal Spirit.

#### Natural Spirit

You emerged from a human concept. You represent an idea, a fear, an aspect of existence that humanity has poured enough dread into to give it a body. You have probably thought more carefully about what it means to be human than most humans have.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Your Intelligence or Charisma score (whichever you did not increase with your base *Ability Score Increase*) increases by 1.

***Conceptual Body.*** Your physical form reflects your foundational concept. Choose one damage type that your concept naturally embodies (fire, cold, necrotic, thunder, etc.). You have immunity to that damage type.

***Domain Inclination.*** Your Domain Expansion, when developed, is guaranteed to manifest a sure-hit effect tied to your concept. When you eventually unlock Domain Expansion, your DM should design its sure-hit in consultation with your foundational concept rather than from a general pool.

***Conceptual Resistance.*** When a creature attempts to identify your innate technique by observing it (such as through the Technique Thief feat), they must succeed on an Intelligence check against your CE Save DC or fail to grasp its underlying logic, even if they witnessed it clearly.

#### Cursed Animal Spirit

You are not born from an idea. You are born from what happened to an animal, from the dread of being hunted, caged, eaten, or forgotten. Animals accumulate cursed energy differently from humans.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Your Strength or Dexterity score increases by 2, in addition to your base *Ability Score Increase*.

***Animal Form.*** Choose the animal your spirit originates from. Your body incorporates clear features of that animal such as fur, scales, feathers, claws, etc. You gain a natural weapon appropriate to your form that deals 1d8 + your Strength modifier in bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage as a CE-infused attack.

***Predator's Senses.*** You have advantage on Perception checks that rely on smell or hearing.

***Territorial Instinct.*** When a creature enters a space within 10 feet of you without your permission, you may use your reaction to make one natural weapon attack against it.

***Pack Logic.*** You do not experience cursed spirits as enemies by default. When encountering a non-sentient cursed spirit, it will not attack you unless you attack first or it is directed to by another creature.

***Wordless Dread.*** Your *Unbounded Malice* trait affects creatures that cannot understand your language.

**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d12 + your Constitution modifier per Warrior level after 1st.

#### Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields.

**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons.

**Saving Throws:** Strength, Constitution.

**Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Survival, and Acrobatics.

#### Cursed Energy

**CE Stat:** Constitution.

**CE Save DC** = 8 + proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier.

**CE Attack Bonus** = proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier.

#### Cursed Flesh
*1st Level*

Your body is reinforced by cursed energy at all times. At the time of creating your character, you choose resistance to one of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage from non-CE-infused attacks. This resistance is lost if you have 0 CE Points.

#### Reckless CE
*1st Level*

When you make a CE-infused melee attack, you may choose to attack recklessly. You gain advantage on that attack roll, but attack rolls against you have advantage until the start of your next turn.

#### Cursed Rage
*1st Level*

You have learned to flood your body with raw cursed energy, bypassing technique entirely. As a bonus action, you may enter a Cursed Rage for up to 1 minute. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

While raging:

- You deal bonus force damage on CE-infused melee attacks equal to half your Constitution modifier (rounded up).
- You have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
- You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from non-CE-infused attacks, in addition to your Cursed Flesh resistance.

The rage ends early if you are incapacitated, if you have not attacked a creature or taken damage since your last turn, or if you choose to end it as a bonus action.

You cannot cast techniques that cost more than 4 CE Points while raging.

\page

#### Pain Threshold
*2nd Level*

When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you may spend 3 CE Points to drop to 1 hit point instead. You may use this feature once per short rest.

#### Even More Cursed Flesh
*3rd Level*

Your resistance from Cursed Flesh now applies to CE-infused attacks that deal the type of damage you chose at 1st level, provided you have at least 3 CE Points remaining.

#### Subclass
*3rd Level*

At 3rd level, choose the Ironclad or Berserker subclass.

#### Ability Score Improvement
*4th,8th,12th,16th,19th Level*

Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1. You cannot exceed 20 in a score this way. Alternatively, pick a non-racial feat, provided you meet the requirements.

#### Extra Attack
*5th Level*

When you take the Attack action, you may attack twice instead of once.

Alternatively, you may forgo one of your attacks to make a single Big-Ass Swing. Make one CE-infused melee attack. On a hit, the attack deals an additional 2d8 force damage and the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your CE Save DC or be knocked prone. You may spend up to 3 CE Points when making a Big-Ass Swing - for each point spent, the additional damage increases by 1d8.

#### Cursed Surge
*5th Level*

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you may spend up to 6 CE Points. For each point spent, you gain 3 temporary hit points and your next attack deals an additional 1d6 force damage. These temporary hit points last until the start of your next turn.

#### Brutal Strikes
*7th Level*

When you score a critical hit with a CE-infused melee attack, the extra dice are maximised rather than rolled. This requires no CE expenditure.

#### CE Resilience
*9th Level*

When you fail a saving throw, you may spend 3 CE Points as a reaction to reroll it. You must use the second result.

\column

#### Relentless Body
*11th Level*

You may use Pain Threshold twice per short rest rather than once. Additionally, while you have at least 1 hit point, you cannot be reduced below 1 hit point more than once per turn.

#### Cursed Surge Evolved
*13th Level*

You may use Cursed Surge twice per long rest.

#### Indomitable Flesh
*15th Level*

Your hit point maximum increases by an amount equal to your Warrior level. You no longer suffer the effects of the exhausted condition until you have 3 or more levels of exhaustion.

#### Unstoppable Force
*17th Level*

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you may declare that your next CE-infused melee attack cannot be reduced, negated, or redirected by any means short of a Domain Expansion.

#### Perfect Body
*20th Level*

Your Constitution score increases by 4, to a maximum of 24. Your CE Points maximum increases by 2 at the start of each combat encounter, lasting until the encounter ends. While you have at least 1 CE Point remaining, you are immune to the frightened and charmed conditions.

*"My name is not cursed spirit... My name is Dagon. Hanami, Jogo and Mahito. WE ALL BEAR NAMES!"*

The following is a chapter focused around enemies that players can encounter, or allies that they are able to summon to aid them in combat. This is not exhaustive and you're more than welcome to modify these as required.

# Chapter 9: Cursed Tools

*"I finally grasped it on the verge of death. The true essence of cursed energy! The reason you’re going to lose is because you didn’t chop my head off! And because you didn’t used that cursed tool when you stabbed me in the head!"*
 
Cursed Tools are objects - weapons, garments, instruments, and miscellaneous items - infused with cursed energy, either passively through long exposure or actively through the deliberate work of a skilled craftsperson. Unlike a sorcerer's innate technique, a cursed tool is external power. It can be lost, stolen, destroyed, or passed from hand to hand.
 
## Using Cursed Tools
 
Any creature capable of perceiving cursed energy may attune to and use a cursed tool. Some tools require a minimum CE Point total to activate their special properties. Others function for anyone who can hold them.
 
### Attunement
 
Cursed tools marked as requiring attunement must be held and focused upon during a short rest to attune. A character may be attuned to a maximum of three cursed tools at once. Breaking attunement requires a bonus action.
 
### CE-Infused Attacks
 
All cursed tools, regardless of special properties, count as CE-infused for the purpose of class features, resistances, and damage to cursed spirits. A non-sorcerer wielding a cursed tool bypasses the immunity of Grade 4 and lower cursed spirits but cannot activate any properties that require CE Point expenditure.
 
### Grades
 
Cursed tools follow the same grading scale as sorcerers and cursed spirits. A tool's grade reflects its rarity, the complexity of its infused properties, and the danger of its misuse.
 
| Tool Grade | Rarity |
|:---|:---|
| Grade 4 | Common |
| Grade 3 | Uncommon |
| Grade 2 | Rare |
| Grade 1 | Very Rare |
| Special Grade | Legendary |
 
\column

## Example Cursed Tools

The following is a list of both canon and non-canon cursed tools that can be used in campaigns. This is not an exhaustive list but more than detailed enough to provide context for DMs creating their own.

### Grade 4 Cursed Tools
 
Grade 4 tools are the workhorses of jujutsu society. Most are simple weapons or practical items with minimal infusion - enough CE to register on a cursed spirit, but nothing that would make a senior sorcerer take special note.
 
#### Reinforced Weapon
*Grade 4 Cursed Tool*
 
A standard melee or ranged weapon - sword, staff, axe, bow - that has been passively infused with cursed energy over time. It carries no special properties, but its CE-infusion allows it to register against spirits and reinforced bodies. Damage, range, etc. based on vanilla weapon.
 
**Properties:** This weapon deals an additional 1d4 force damage on a hit against cursed spirits and CE-reinforced creatures.
 
#### Binding Thread
*Grade 4 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** As an action, spend 1 CE Point to affix the thread across any opening or to an object. If a creature passes through the opening or disturbs the object, you are immediately aware of it regardless of distance, provided you are within 1 mile.
 
#### Sight Spectacles
*Grade 4 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** While worn, the user can see cursed spirits, CE signatures, and active barriers regardless of their own CE level. The wearer can perceive the approximate grade of any CE signature within 30 feet. Does not grant any other sorcerer ability.
 
#### Standard-Issue Exorcism Blade
*Grade 4 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** Deals 1d6 slashing damage + 1d4 force damage. Against Grade 4 or lower cursed spirits, this weapon deals maximum damage on a hit.

#### Cursed Bandages
*Grade 4 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** While worn, at the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. Once per long rest, you may spend 4 CE Points as a reaction to regain 1d6 hit points.
 
\page
 
### Grade 3 Cursed Tools
 
Grade 3 tools carry active properties - a deliberate infusion of technique, effect, or cursed energy behaviour beyond simple reinforcement. A Grade 3 operative equipped with a Grade 3 tool is considered appropriately resourced for most field assignments.
 
#### Battle Axe (Standard Grade)
*Grade 3 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** Deals 1d12 slashing + 1d6 force damage. When you hit a cursed spirit with this weapon, it cannot use its movement to retreat until the start of its next turn. On a critical hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 14) or be knocked prone.
 
#### Wrapped Blade
*Grade 3 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** Deals 1d8 piercing + 1d6 force damage. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with this weapon, you may spend 2 CE Points to discharge the stored energy. The target takes an additional 2d8 force damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC = your CE Save DC) or be pushed 10 feet directly away from you.
 
#### Cursed Rope
*Grade 3 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** As an action, spend 3 CE Points to throw the rope at a creature within 20 feet. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw against your CE Save DC or be restrained until the end of their next turn. While restrained by the rope, any technique the target attempts that costs 3 or fewer CE Points is automatically nullified.
 
#### Cursed Scroll
*Grade 3 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** When you use this scroll as an action, roll 1d6 and consult the table below. The effect uses your CE Save DC.
 
| d6 | Effect |
|:---:|:---|
| 1/2 | All creatures within 15 feet take 3d8 force damage (Con save for half). |
| 3/4 | One creature within 30 feet is restrained for 1 minute (Str save ends). |
| 5 | You and up to two allies within 15 feet regain 2d6 + 4 CE Points. |
| 6 | A barrier surrounds a 20-foot radius area centred on you for 1 hour. Cursed spirits cannot cross it. |

#### Crow Familiars
*Grade 3 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** As a bonus action, spend 2 CE Points to launch one familiar at a target within 60 feet. Make a CE attack roll. On a hit, deal 2d6 force damage. You may launch all three in one action by spending 6 CE Points, making a separate attack roll for each.

\column

#### Spiked Chain
*Grade 3 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** Deals 1d8 bludgeoning + 1d4 force damage. When you hit a creature with this weapon, it cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn. On a critical hit the target is also knocked prone.
 
### Grade 2 Cursed Tools
 
Grade 2 tools are institutional assets. They are logged, tracked, and assigned rather than purchased. Finding one in the hands of a curse user is considered a significant intelligence failure.
 
#### Dragon-Bone
*Grade 2 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** Deals 1d10 piercing + 1d6 force damage. Dragon-Bone has three Charge engines. At the start of each of your turns in combat, you gain one Charge (maximum 3). As a bonus action, you may discharge any number of Charges. For each Charge discharged, the next attack with Dragon-Bone deals an additional 2d8 force damage. Charges reset to 0 at the end of combat.
 
#### Split Soul Katana
*Grade 2 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** Deals 1d8 slashing damage. Ignore all resistances and immunities to damage from this weapon except those held by creatures without souls. On a critical hit, target takes maximum damage from this weapon and their RCT is disabled for 24 hours.

Any damage dealt by this weapon cannot be healed by Reverse Cursed Technique or standard rest. It can only be recovered through healing by spending 8 CE Points and 10 minutes of healing.

#### Sealed Gourd
*Grade 2 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** As an action, break the seal to release the spirit. A dice roll determines the grade (1d4: 1/2 = Grade 3, 3 = Grade 2, 4 = Grade 1). The spirit is hostile to all creatures. Once released, this item is destroyed.

### Grade 1 Cursed Tools
 
Grade 1 tools are rare enough that most working sorcerers will never handle one. They are held in institutional armories, sealed in clan vaults, or lost somewhere in the long history of the jujutsu world.
 
#### Black Rope
*Grade 1 Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** When you strike a creature with Black Rope, that creature must make a Constitution saving throw against your CE Save DC. On a failure, any active technique on or by that creature with a CE cost of 6 or lower is immediately ended. On a success, techniques with a CE cost of 3 or lower are ended.
Black Rope has 10 charges, and each use expends 1 charge. It cannot be repaired once its charges are exhausted.

\page
 
#### Gavel of Judgement
*Grade 1 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** Deals 2d6 bludgeoning + 2d6 force damage. When you hit a creature with this weapon, you may spend 4 CE Points to declare a Verdict. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your CE Save DC. On a failure, choose one:
 
- The target drops any held items and cannot pick them up until the end of its next turn.
- The target is silenced for 1 minute. It may repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns.
- The target loses CE Points equal to your proficiency bonus.
 
#### Cursed Electric Guitar
*Grade 1 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** As an action, you may release a cursed sound wave in a 30-foot cone. All creatures in the cone must make a Constitution saving throw against your CE Save DC or take 4d8 thunder damage and be deafened until the end of their next turn. On success, they take half damage and are not deafened.
 
If you have proficiency in a musical instrument, you may spend 3 CE Points when activating this property to shape the wave. Choose one of the following effects:

- Double the cone's length to 60 feet
- Affect all creatures in a 20-foot radius instead of a cone
- Force creatures that fail by 5 or more to be stunned rather than deafened.

#### Mourning Cloth
*Grade 1 Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** While worn, cursed spirits must make a Wisdom saving throw against your CE Save DC before making their first attack against you each combat. On a failure, they must target a different creature that turn. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against fear effects and death saving throws.

### Special Grade Cursed Tools
 
Special Grade tools appear in the historical record, vanish, and reappear somewhere unexpected, usually in the middle of a situation that was already bad. Most sorcerers will encounter at most one in their career.

#### Playful Cloud
*Special Grade Cursed Tool, requires attunement*
 
**Properties:** Deals 2d10 bludgeoning damage. Add your Strength modifier twice to damage rolls with this weapon instead of once. Playful Cloud ignores resistances and immunities to bludgeoning damage.
 
When you score a critical hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your CE Save DC. On a failure, it is stunned until the end of its next turn and is knocked prone. On a success, it's only knocked prone.
 
Playful Cloud does not lose its properties when the wielder reaches 0 CE Points. A Fully Restricted wielder (or one with a Complete Heavenly Restriction) using Playful Cloud adds their Strength modifier three times to damage rolls instead of twice.

#### Inverted Spear of Heaven
*Special Grade Cursed Tool*
 
**Properties:** When you hit a creature with the Inverted Spear of Heaven, any active technique on that creature - including passive reinforcements, barriers, and persistent effects - is immediately ended. ## Crafting Cursed Tools
 
Cursed tools don't appear from nowhere. They're made slowly, carefully, and at significant personal cost. A sorcerer with the time, materials, and knowledge may attempt to create one.
 
### Requirements
 
Crafting a cursed tool requires the following:
 
- A base object appropriate to the intended tool (a weapon, instrument, garment, etc.)
- A number of days of uninterrupted work equal to the tool's grade (Grade 4 = 4 days, Grade 3 = 8 days, Grade 2 = 20 days, Grade 1 = 60 days)
- A CE expenditure made at the start of each day of crafting, as shown on the table below
- A successful CE check at the end of the crafting period

##### Crafting Requirements by Grade
| Grade | Daily CE Cost | Crafting DC | Min. Sorcerer Grade |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 4 | 4 CE Points | 12 | Grade 4 |
| 3 | 6 CE Points | 14 | Grade 3 |
| 2 | 10 CE Points | 16 | Grade 2 |
| 1 | 16 CE Points | 19 | Grade 1 |
| Special | 24 CE Points | 23 | Special Grade |
 
The CE check is made using the crafter's CE Stat modifier + proficiency bonus. On a failure, the tool is created but gains a flaw (determined by the DM) that cannot be removed.
 
Special Grade tools cannot be deliberately created and emerge from extraordinary circumstances: the death of a Special Grade sorcerer, a catastrophic technique discharge, or conditions that cannot be reliably reproduced.
 
### Degradation
 
Some cursed tools degrade with use. Any tool with limited charges that reaches 0 charges becomes a mundane object - the CE infusion is spent. At the DM's discretion, a skilled craftsperson may attempt to re-infuse a depleted tool, treating it as a new crafting project of one grade lower than the original.

### Modification

Existing cursed tools can be modified from their original intended use, though doing so is significantly more unstable than creating a tool from scratch.

Modifying a cursed tool requires:

* Access to the original tool
* Half the normal crafting time required for a tool of its grade
* Daily CE expenditure equal to the tool's normal crafting cost
* A successful CE check at the end of the process

A modified tool may have one of the following changes applied:

* Change its physical form (such as a sword into chained blades, or gauntlets into greaves)
* Alter its damage type
* Replace one property or effect with another of similar power
* Reduce or increase its CE efficiency
* Add a conditional activation requirement

The modification DC equals the tool's normal crafting DC +2. If the modification fundamentally changes the tool's intended purpose, increase the DC by an additional +2.

On a failed modification check, roll 1d6:

|  d6 | Modification Failure                                                   |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | The tool loses all cursed properties and becomes mundane               |
|  2  | The tool gains a permanent flaw                                        |
|  3  | CE costs of the tool increase by 2 permanently                         |
|  4  | The tool becomes unstable and deals damage to its wielder on use       |
|  5  | The modification only partially succeeds                               |
|  6  | The modification succeeds |

A cursed tool may only safely undergo a number of successful modifications equal to its grade before its CE structure becomes unstable. Further modifications automatically impose disadvantage on all crafting checks related to the tool.

Special Grade tools resist modification entirely.

# Chapter 10: Creating a Character

## Making Your Character

Finally, we get to the fun part. Follow the steps below to create a 1st-level character, and complete each step in order.

### Step 1 - Ability Scores

Assign the following array to your six ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) in any order:

**17, 15, 14, 12, 10, 8**

Apply racial modifiers after assigning the array.

### Step 2 - Race

Choose one race from the module and gain it's racial fetures. You also gain the subcategory features, with some specified additions to existing racial features.

### Step 3 - Class

Choose one class from the relevant chapter of this document. Your CE Stat is determined by your class, so choose wisely.

### Step 4 - Background

Choose one background from the  module. Your background provides:

- Two skill proficiencies
- Two tool or language proficiencies
- A background feature
- Starting equipment and Yen

Backgrounds from other sourcebooks (aside from those in the PHB that make sense for the setting) are not permitted without DM approval.

### Step 5 - Free Feature

You gain one additional feature of your choice, such as:

- A standard feat you meet the prerequisites for
- A racial feat you meet the prerequisites for
- A 1st-level class feature from a class other than your own (yes, really)

### Step 6 - Equipment

You begin play with all equipment granted by your background and class. If your starting equipment does not suit your character concept, speak with your DM. Additional equipment may be available, or you may use your starting Yen to purchase items instead.

> ##### Starting Yen
> The amount of starting Yen available to you is determined by your background. Consult your DM if your background does not specify a Yen value.
